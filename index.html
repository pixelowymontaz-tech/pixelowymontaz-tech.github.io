Hi, I'm Ignacy.

I'm 13 and I'm building a 360° camera from scratch.

I design the electronics and the PCB myself. Later I'll also write the software that stitches the images from two lenses into one panorama. I'm learning everything as I go, so plenty of things don't work the first time.

This is where I write down what I did, what didn't work and what's next.
